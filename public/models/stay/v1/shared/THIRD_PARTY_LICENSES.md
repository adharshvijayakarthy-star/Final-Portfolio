| Original source | Creator | Delivered adaptation |
|---|---|---|
| [Tree Small 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/tree_small_02) | Rico Cilliers | Reduced trunk meshes, sampled crown distributions and cropped diffuse/opacity leaf sprays. Used in G01, G03, G04, G11 and F01. Sakura flowers are newly authored geometry. |
| [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02) | Rob Tuytel | Shared bark color, normal and roughness images. |
| [Mossy Rock](https://polyhaven.com/a/mossy_rock) | Rob Tuytel | Recolored stone diffuse plus reduced normal/roughness images. |
| [Forest Ground 04](https://polyhaven.com/a/forest_ground_04) | Rob Tuytel; Rico Cilliers | Recolored moss/earth diffuse plus reduced normal/roughness images. |
| [Wood Planks Grey](https://polyhaven.com/a/wood_planks_grey) | Rob Tuytel | Warm cedar recoloring plus reduced normal/roughness images. |

Sources retrieved 2026-09-26. Desktop PBR maps are 512 square; mobile maps are 256 square. Leaf sprays are 256×512 desktop and 128×256 mobile. GLBs reference the adjacent shared images by relative URI. Preserve this folder when moving the package.

No original high-resolution third-party model is required to load the delivered GLBs or the packed Blender masters. Acquisition copies and the recoverable entry assets remain in the local `.aura-work/senior-art-pass` working directory. Detailed production provenance is in `scripts/blender/stay-awhile/senior-provenance.json` and each master's `READ_ME` text block.
