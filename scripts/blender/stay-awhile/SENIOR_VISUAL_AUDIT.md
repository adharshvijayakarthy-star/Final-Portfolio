## Explicit limits

The mobile LODs retain silhouettes and material families with fewer leaves and lower-resolution maps. Close card edges and angular opaque Sakura petals remain visible at some angles. The report does not claim botanical photorealism or hide these budget tradeoffs.

The material-detail camera is a review-only view. The wide/primary/landmark/late-close views use the authored camera-path function; reverse looks back from a review position and mobile uses the mobile path variant. No source camera guide was moved.

The source renders and representative GLB renders were inspected as images. Structural, motion, rail and import checks are separate evidence; they do not prove browser performance or visual parity with a particular web renderer.
