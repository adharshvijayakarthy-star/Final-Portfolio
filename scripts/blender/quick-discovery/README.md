# Quick Discovery master

`quick-discovery-master.blend` owns the fourteen-stage scroll timeline. The five project performances retain their original internal choreography; THINK and LEAD insert two slots before WORK. `frame()` translates legacy stage numbers so their keyframes remain synchronized.

Run `blender --background --python scripts/blender/quick-discovery/create-master.py` to regenerate, then the same command with `-- --export`. Copy the two GLBs and motion-manifest.json from `exported/` into `public/models/quick-discovery/` together. No video is used on the website.

The six handoffs are authored in `spatial_handoffs()`. Camera, geometry and light samples are driven by the same reversible scroll clock. The browser applies responsive framing, the enlarged graph's horizontal offset and periphery visibility. Embers keep their independent loop. Certificate images and accessible dialogs remain HTML; the ISSO image is explicitly a placeholder pending the real scan.

The reference recording's ember field, foreground facets and crimson continuity object are retained. The logger alone omits the unrelated crimson ring. Reduced motion uses the semantic document without camera travel.
